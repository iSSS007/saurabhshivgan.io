Meeting Tour

With Sumedha

💐 🌸 💐

Scene One · The Arrival

She walked in 🚶‍♀️

  • 01I reached early. Sat there alone, checking my phone every 2 minutes like it would help.
  • 02 Simple entry, big effect on me.
  • 03She looked calm. I looked calm on the outside. Inside — total chaos.
  • 04We did that awkward hi-smile thing.
  • 05I said something like "glad you came" — very boring line, I know.
  • 06Walking to the table I was thinking: walk normal, don't overthink, walk normal — classic me.
  • 07She sat down easy, looked around — and somehow the whole restaurant felt less scary after that.

Scene Two · The Conversation

We talked, I tried 😅

  • 01Menu आला, आम्ही दोघे त्यात लपलो for a moment. Classic first-meeting move.
  • 02She asked a real question — not a boring "what do you do?" but something that actually wanted an answer.
  • 03I was honest. No showing off, no big stories. Just plain Saurabh — जसा आहे तसा.
  • 04Some moments I went quiet and had nothing to say. It happens with me.
  • 05But she never made me feel bad about it. She just picked up the topic and kept going — very kind of her.
  • 06She shared something personal too. I listened properly — मन लावून — not just waiting for my turn to talk.
  • 07जेवण आलं but we were still talking. That's a good sign I think — food can wait, good company can't.

Scene Three · The Laughter

Something was funny 😄

  • 01Something came up and we both laughed at the exact same second. No planning.
  • 02Her laugh was real. Not polite-smile kind. Full genuine laugh. मला खूप आवडलं ते.
  • 03I threw a small joke out there, not sure if it'll land. It landed. Relief + happiness, both at once.
  • 04When two people laugh together — something shifts. The awkward first-meeting feeling just goes away.
  • 05Normally I'm not the funniest person. But with her, a more relaxed version of me showed up. कसं काय झालं माहीत नाही.
  • 06After the laugh, the talk became softer. More open. Less "interview", more "two people actually talking."
  • 07This scene is my favourite to remember. just that one shared laugh. साधं पण खरं.

Scene Four · Leaving the Restaurant

Time to go 🚪

  • 01Bill आला. That moment when a good evening starts wrapping up and you're not fully ready.
  • 02Walking out, I noticed we were going a little slow. Neither of us was rushing. Small good sign.
  • 03Pune's evening air outside — ती थंड हवा — and the chat became lighter, easier.
  • 04Internally I knew I had been boring at some points. Quiet when I should have been fun.
  • 05But she was patient the whole evening. More patient than I deserved, honestly.
  • 06We talked about something small — I don't even remember what — just two people not saying bye yet.
  • 07Parking कडे चालत गेलो. I had one last thing in my bag. Waiting for the right moment all evening.

Scene Five · The Parking Goodbye

Flowers & GoodByes 💙

  • 01तिच्या scooter जवळ — I pulled out the blue orchids and a Dairy Milk. Held them out. Simple moment.
  • 02Blue orchids are not a common gift. I picked them because they're a little different. तिच्यासारखंच
  • 03Dairy Milk because it's just sweet and simple. No big statement .
  • 04We said bye. Then kept talking. Then said bye again. That second bye — that's always the real one.
  • 05My bike was far. I walked across the parking. Looked back — ती थांबली होती. Still there. Watching.
  • 06She didn't have to wait. Her scooter was right there. But ती थांबली — and that meant everything.
  • 07मी — Saurabh, nervous, sometimes boring, dark-brown guy — rode home with one thought: she waited.
